Privacy policy – Revenue Collector 48

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What the app accesses

With my permission, Revenue Collector 48 uses the Google AdMob API with read-only access (admob.readonly) to read the reporting data of my own AdMob account: earnings, impressions, ad requests and similar totals per app, ad unit and country. It cannot change anything in the account.

How the data is used and stored

The access token is stored only on my own computer. The report totals are sent to a private, internal reporting database that only I and my team's administrators can read. No personal data about app users is accessed. The data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
